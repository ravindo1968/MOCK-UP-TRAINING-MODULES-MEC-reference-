RPS Interaktif

Klik setiap baris untuk melihat detail topik, metode pengajaran, dan indikator ketercapaian.

Minggu Topik Subtopik Indikator Durasi Metode
Wk 1–2 Maritime English Fundamentals Basic vocabulary, phonetics, STCW terms Mampu menyebutkan 200+ kosa kata teknis 6 jam Blended
Wk 3–4 Bridge Communication COLREGS, VHF phraseology, reporting Simulasi komunikasi bridge-to-bridge 6 jam Simulasi
Wk 5–6 Engine Room Language Technical terms, maintenance English Menulis laporan teknis dalam bahasa Inggris 6 jam Workshop
Wk 7–8 GMDSS & Safety Communication Mayday, Pan-Pan, distress signals Role-play prosedur darurat lengkap 6 jam Role-Play
Wk 9–10 Port State Control (PSC) English Inspection procedures, documentation Mampu menjawab pertanyaan PSC officer 6 jam Mock Drill
Wk 11–12 Cargo Operations Language Loading, stowage, Bill of Lading Membuat cargo manifest dalam bahasa Inggris 6 jam Case Study
Wk 13–14 Medical & First Aid English Medical terminology, MFAG communication Komunikasi darurat medis via radio 6 jam Simulasi
Wk 15–16 Job Interview Preparation CV, cover letter, mock interview Lolos seleksi crewing agency internasional 6 jam Interview
